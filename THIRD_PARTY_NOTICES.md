The PNG, PDF and DOCX fixtures are generated by `scripts/generate_fixtures.py`.
`scripts/generate_video.py` produces original bitmap cards and mathematical sine-wave tones.
No downloaded video, system-voice recording, external font or private user file is included.
The generated MP4 stays ignored; users can regenerate it with their installed ffmpeg.
Public-video URLs in manual tests remain links to their owners' hosted media.

The installation conventions and MIT license follow this author's
[DeepSeek Raycast Adapter](https://github.com/Johnnyzlee/raycast-deepseek-adapter).
No third-party adapter implementation has been vendored.
